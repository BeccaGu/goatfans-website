Teddy Swims

Introducing a release without losing the artist behind it.

For Teddy Swims' “God Went Crazy”, we worked on a release collaboration designed for GOATFANS' Chinese audience.

Rather than treating the track as a standalone promotional message, the content placed the release within a wider artist story — giving listeners a reason to understand the person and music behind the campaign.

Artist campaign / New release / Chinese content

02 /

Cleo Sol

Watch on YouTube ↗

Building context around an artist.

Cleo Sol already had a growing audience in China, but much of the conversation around her music was still happening organically.

GOATFANS created a long-form piece exploring her music, story and the world around it — giving Chinese listeners more context to discover the artist beyond individual tracks.

It reflects the kind of work we do best: finding the story around the music, translating its cultural context, and turning existing curiosity into a deeper connection with Chinese listeners.

Artist storytelling / Editorial / China audience development

03 /

Fender

Watch on YouTube ↗

Why does a song feel different when the bass disappears?

For Fender's 75th anniversary of the Precision Bass, we created an original GOATFANS story around a simple idea: bass matters.

Rather than beginning with the product, we began with the music — looking at the role bass plays in the records people love and why its absence can completely change the way a song feels.

The Fender Precision Bass became part of that wider story.

The video reached more than 120,000 views on Bilibili, showing how a product can sit naturally inside music-led editorial when the idea comes first.

Brand collaboration / Original concept / Long-form video / Bilibili
