GOATFANS is an independent music platform and agency connecting international artists with listeners in China.

We started by making the kind of music content we wanted to see ourselves — digging into the records, artists and sounds we love, and sharing them with a growing community of music listeners across China.

Today, we use that same audience, taste and local knowledge to help international artists find their footing in the Chinese market.
