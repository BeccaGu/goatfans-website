Services

What we do

There is no single way into China.

Every artist arrives with a different audience, story and ambition. We start with the music, work out where the opportunity is, and build from there.

01

Digital Strategy & PR

Build the foundations first.

You don't need to be in China to start building an audience here. We help artists and their teams understand where their music sits in the Chinese market, how it should be introduced, and which platforms and communities make the most sense.

  • China market and audience positioning
  • Chinese artist biographies and EPK localisation
  • Release and campaign strategy
  • Chinese press releases
  • Music media outreach
  • Platform strategy across Bilibili, RED, Douyin and Chinese music platforms
  • Building a long-term, searchable Chinese digital presence

Localisation, to us, isn't simply translation.

A biography that works in London, Berlin or New York won't necessarily tell the same story in China. We look at the references, context and conversations around an artist and find a way of introducing them that makes sense to Chinese listeners — without losing what made the artist interesting in the first place.

02

Content & Creator Campaigns

Give people a reason to care.

GOATFANS began as a music content platform, and content remains at the heart of what we do. We create stories around artists and records rather than treating music promotion as advertising.

  • Original GOATFANS editorial content
  • Artist interviews and conversations
  • Record and release features
  • Short and long-form video
  • Bilibili, RED and Douyin campaigns
  • Creator collaborations
  • Music community seeding
  • Campaign concepts built specifically for Chinese audiences

Sometimes the best way to introduce an artist isn't to tell people to stream a new single.

It might be the story behind a record, an unusual production choice, an influence, an instrument, a live performance, or simply the reason the music matters. That's the part we like figuring out.

03

Live & China Visits

Take the relationship offline.

For artists planning to visit China, we can help explore opportunities beyond digital promotion. Rather than jumping straight into a large tour, the right first step may be a small showcase, listening event, collaboration or appearance that introduces the artist to local listeners and the wider music community.

  • Showcase planning
  • Venue and event research
  • Promoter and local partner introductions
  • China live-market guidance
  • Local campaign support around confirmed shows
  • Artist appearances and collaborations
  • Tour and itinerary consultation

Travel, accommodation, visas, venue hire and other production costs are normally handled separately depending on the project.

How we work

Start with the music.

01

Send us your music

Tell us a little about the artist, the release and what you're hoping to do in China. An EPK is useful, but a few links and a good introduction are enough to start.

02

We listen

We look at the music, existing audience, story, release plans and any signs of interest already coming from China. Sometimes the right answer is to wait.

03

We talk

If we think there's something worth exploring, we'll talk about the artist, the opportunity and what a sensible first step might look like.

04

We build the right campaign

PR, GOATFANS content, a wider creator campaign, a China market test, live support — or a combination of them.
