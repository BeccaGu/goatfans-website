What we do

There is no single way into China.

Every artist arrives with a different audience, story and ambition. We start with the music, work out where the opportunity is, and build from there.

Our work covers digital PR, original content, creator collaborations and live opportunities — with a focus on helping independent artists build something meaningful rather than simply being present on another platform.

01 / SERVICES

Digital Strategy & PR

Build the foundations first.

You don't need to be in China to start building an audience here.

We help artists and their teams understand where their music sits in the Chinese market, how it should be introduced, and which platforms and communities make the most sense.

This can include:

  • China market and audience positioning
  • Chinese artist biographies and EPK localisation
  • Release and campaign strategy
  • Chinese press releases
  • Music media outreach
  • Platform strategy across Bilibili, RED, Douyin and Chinese music platforms
  • Building a long-term, searchable Chinese digital presence

Localisation, to us, isn't simply translation.

A biography that works in London, Berlin or New York won't necessarily tell the same story in China. We look at the references, context and conversations around an artist and find a way of introducing them that makes sense to Chinese listeners — without losing what made the artist interesting in the first place.

02 / SERVICES

Content & Creator Campaigns

Give people a reason to care.

GOATFANS began as a music content platform, and content remains at the heart of what we do.

We create stories around artists and records rather than treating music promotion as advertising.

Depending on the project, this might include:

  • Original GOATFANS editorial content
  • Artist interviews and conversations
  • Record and release features
  • Short and long-form video
  • Bilibili, RED and Douyin campaigns
  • Creator collaborations
  • Music community seeding
  • Campaign concepts built specifically for Chinese audiences

Sometimes the best way to introduce an artist isn't to tell people to stream a new single.

It might be the story behind a record, an unusual production choice, an influence, an instrument, a live performance, or simply the reason the music matters.

That's the part we like figuring out.

03 / SERVICES

Live & China Visits

Take the relationship offline.

For artists planning to visit China, we can help explore opportunities beyond digital promotion.

Rather than jumping straight into a large tour, the right first step may be a small showcase, listening event, collaboration or appearance that introduces the artist to local listeners and the wider music community.

Depending on the project, we can help with:

  • Showcase planning
  • Venue and event research
  • Promoter and local partner introductions
  • China live-market guidance
  • Local campaign support around confirmed shows
  • Artist appearances and collaborations
  • Tour and itinerary consultation

Travel, accommodation, visas, venue hire and other production costs are normally handled separately depending on the project.

04 / SERVICES

How we work

01 — Send us your music

Tell us a little about the artist, the release and what you're hoping to do in China.

An EPK is useful, but a few links and a good introduction are enough to start.

02 — We listen

Before suggesting anything, we listen.

We look at the music, existing audience, story, release plans and any signs of interest already coming from China.

Not every artist needs the same campaign — and sometimes the right answer is to wait.

03 — We talk

If we think there's something worth exploring, we'll arrange a conversation about the artist, the opportunity and what a sensible first step might look like.

04 — We build the right campaign

We put together a proposal around the actual project.

That might be a focused PR campaign, an original piece of GOATFANS content, a wider creator campaign, a China market test, live support — or a combination of them.

No unnecessary package. No copy-and-paste campaign.

05 / SERVICES

When should we talk?

For digital PR, we recommend getting in touch around 8–10 weeks before release.

For a wider release campaign, around three months gives us more room to develop the story, content and outreach properly.

For a China showcase, headline show or multi-city visit, six months or more is preferable.

Working on a shorter timeline? Send it over anyway. We'll tell you what is realistically possible.

06 / SERVICES

Where we work

Our main focus is China's music and social ecosystem, including:

Bilibili — long-form video, music culture and highly engaged communities
RED / Xiaohongshu — discovery, taste and lifestyle-led conversations
Douyin — short-form discovery and cultural momentum
NetEase Cloud Music & Tencent Music platforms — listening, artist discovery and fandom

We don't believe every artist needs to be everywhere.

The point is to find where the music belongs.
